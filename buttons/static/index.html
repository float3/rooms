Red or blue

For a room of people

Everyone presses red or blue

If more than half press blue, everybody lives. If not, only the ones who pressed red do.

How it goes

  • Everyone presses one of two buttons on their phone. Nobody sees who pressed what until you reveal the round.
  • Red lives whatever the room does. Blue lives only if enough of the room presses it too.
  • You set the share blue has to beat: half by default. Exactly that share is not enough.
  • Whoever presses nothing is not counted.
